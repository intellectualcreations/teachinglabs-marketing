Got it! Adding fractions with different denominators (the bottom numbers). Let's break it down step by step.

Say you want to add 1/3 + 1/4.

First question: can you add them if the bottom numbers are different?
A
No? You need them to be the same?
M
Exactly right! You need a common denominator. Think of it like this — you can't add thirds and fourths because they're different-sized pieces. 🍕

So we need to find a number that both 3 and 4 can divide into evenly. What do you think that number might be?
A
12?
M
Yes! 12 works great. Now we convert:

• 1/3 = 4/12 (multiply top and bottom by 4)
• 1/4 = 3/12 (multiply top and bottom by 3)

Now add them: 4/12 + 3/12 = ?
A
7/12!
M
🎉 You got it! 7/12 is correct. See, you DO get fractions.
